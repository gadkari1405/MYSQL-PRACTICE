# 🚀 DAY 50: E-Commerce Sales Analysis System

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is part of my **50 Days SQL Challenge**, where I built an E-Commerce Sales Analysis System using MySQL. The project demonstrates database design, data cleaning, transformation, KPI reporting, customer analysis, product analysis, revenue analysis, and advanced SQL techniques to generate actionable business insights.
---
# 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze sales data and transform raw business information into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---
# 📊 Top 10 Business Insights

### 1. Top Revenue-Generating Customers
Identified the customers contributing the highest revenue, helping the business prioritize high-value customers for retention and loyalty programs.

### 2. Best-Selling Products
Analyzed product sales to determine the highest-selling products, enabling better inventory planning and marketing strategies.

### 3. Least-Selling Products
Identified products with low sales performance, helping the business optimize inventory and improve product offerings.

### 4. Total Revenue Analysis
Calculated the overall revenue generated from customer orders to evaluate business performance.

### 5. Average Order Value (AOV)
Measured the average amount spent per order, providing insights into customer purchasing behavior and sales effectiveness.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trends
Analyzed monthly sales and revenue trends to identify seasonal demand and business growth patterns.

### 7. Customer Purchase Behavior
Examined customer order frequency to identify repeat customers and understand purchasing habits.

### 8. Product Revenue Contribution
Determined which products generated the highest revenue, helping prioritize profitable products and optimize sales strategies.

### 9. Order Performance Analysis
Analyzed order volume over time to identify peak sales periods and improve operational planning.

### 10. Query Optimization with CTEs
Applied Common Table Expressions (CTEs) to simplify complex SQL queries, making reports more readable, maintainable, and efficient.
